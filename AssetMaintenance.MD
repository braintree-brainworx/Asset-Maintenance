# Asset Maintenance
The Asset Maintenance module provides tools to plan and carry out maintenance activities for fixed assets owned by the business.  
Maintenance activities are recorded out on a Maintenance order. This can be generated for 
- adhoc repairs - where unplanned damage or failure occurs
- from a maintenance plan - to handle routine servicing, based on a time schedule or on asset usage.
